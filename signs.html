How this works

1
Find a place. Pick a ⭐ loop or anything on the list that isn't marked Claimed.
2
Go ask. Start the conversation your own way and show them the example signs on your phone.
3
Got a yes? Tap “Report a Sign.” Pick your name, type the business, tap the status. Done. Then reach out to Coach Brooks to set up a time to pick up the form and check.

📲 Save this to your home screen: iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android, tap the menu, then Add to Home screen. It works like an app after that.

Quick Start

WAYS TO START THE CONVERSATION · NO SCRIPT NEEDED

Know the place: “Hey, I come here all the time. Is the owner or manager around?”

Local angle: “I play baseball at CCRI right up the road. Could I grab the manager for a minute?”

Returning sponsor: “You guys had a sign on our field last year. I wanted to say thanks and see about this year.”

Compliment: “Love this place. Is the owner in today? I've got a quick question about our baseball team.”

Be yourself. Say who you are, why you're there, and ask if they'd have any interest.

Before you walk in

  • Wear CCRI gear, phone away, smile, look them in the eye.
  • Bring 2–3 sponsor forms and a pen.
  • Have the example signs and the sponsor flyer ready on your phone.
  • Ask for the owner or manager. Keep it under a minute.
  • Say thank you either way.

Best times to go

  • Restaurants and bars: weekdays 2–4 pm. Never during lunch or dinner rush, and skip Friday and Saturday nights.
  • Trades and offices (gray “Call” tags): call in the morning, 8–10 am.
  • Shops and activities: weekday afternoons.

What to say

Walk-in (new business)

“Hi, I'm [name], I play baseball at CCRI. Is the owner or manager around for a minute?”
“Thanks. Our team is raising money for our spring trip, and we sell field signs on our fence. It's $425 the first year, which includes making the sign, then just $200 a year after that to keep it up. They're up all year, so they're seen at our home games and all summer during the travel baseball tournaments we host. We'll also thank you on the CCRI baseball Instagram. Would [business] have any interest in a sign?”

Returning sponsor

“Hi, I'm [name] from CCRI baseball. [Business] had a sign on our field last year, and we really appreciated it. I'm here to see if you'd have any interest in renewing it. It's $200, it's seen at our games and all summer at the travel tournaments we host, and we'll thank you on our Instagram.”

Phone or text

“Hi, this is [name], a baseball player at CCRI. We sell field signs on our fence, seen at our games and all summer at the travel tournaments we host, to help pay for our spring trip. It's $425 the first year including the sign, then $200 a year. Can I stop by this week to talk about [business] getting one?”

Follow-up (once, a few days later)

“Hi, it's [name] from CCRI baseball. I stopped by [day] about a field sign. Just checking if you had any questions.”

If they say…

“How much?” “$425 the first year, which includes making the sign. Then $200 a year after that.” (Renewals: $200.)

“What would the sign look like?” “Whatever you want. It can be your logo, a business card design, or how close you are to campus. It's up to you.”

“I need to ask the owner.” “No problem. When's the best time to come back?”

“Send me something.” “Sure. What's the best email or number?” Then send them the sponsor flyer.

“Not this year.” “Totally understand, thanks for your time.”

“Yes!” “Awesome, thank you! I have a quick form for you, and checks are made out to CCRI Athletics.”

After a yes ✅

  • Sponsor form filled out
  • Check made out to CCRI Athletics ($425 new, $200 renewal)
  • Ask how they want the sign to look (logo, business card, close to campus)
  • Tap Report a Sign
  • Reach out to Coach Brooks to set up a time to pick up the form and check
