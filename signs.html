🗣️ What to say

Ask for the owner or manager:
“Hi, I'm ___, I play baseball at CCRI. We sell field signs to pay for our spring trip. Would you have any interest?”

Price: $425 first year (sign included), $200 a year after. Renewals $200.
Seen: spring home games, summer travel tournaments and the fall season, plus an Instagram shout-out.
Their sign, their design.

Yes? Hand them the flyer: form on the back. Payment: cash, check to CCRI Athletics, or Venmo @Jbrooks4392. Make sure your name is on the form. Then report it.

Owner not in? Leave a flyer and ask when to come back. No? “Thanks for your time.” Report it anyway.
