Cheat sheet: what to say ›
  1. Walk in: “Hi, I'm ___, I play baseball at CCRI. Is the owner or manager around?”
  2. Hand the flyer: “We're doing fence signs for our field. Would you have any interest?”
  3. Why it's worth it: $425 first year (sign included), $200/yr after · seen at home games + summer tournaments · Instagram shout-out · their sign, their design
  4. They say yes: Fill out the form on the back + pay: cash, check to CCRI Athletics, or Venmo (QR on the flyer). Make sure your name is on it.
  5. Owner not in: Leave the flyer: “When's a good time to come back?”
  6. They say no: “No problem, thanks for your time!” It still counts.
  7. Report it: Report every visit on the site: yes, maybe, or no.
